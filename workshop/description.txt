[h1]Mod Menu[/h1]
One in-game menu for every mod's settings and hotkeys. Press [b]F11[/b] in the main menu or in game.

[list]
[*][b]Every mod in one list[/b]: each mod with a BepInEx config shows up on the left, its settings on the right.
[*][b]Change settings in place[/b]: On/Off switches, - / + for numbers (Shift for big steps, or click the number and type), choices, text. [b]Default[/b] resets a setting.
[*][b]All keys[/b]: every mod's hotkeys on one page. Click one and press the new key. Keys that two mods share (or that the game uses) show in red.
[*]A short notice at startup if two mods use the same key.
[/list]

Changes save to the mod's .cfg file straight away. Most apply at once; a few need a restart.

Required by Kamikaze's ZED ZONE mods (AmmoStacks, DogShare, Pack Rat, Boltgun, Build Anywhere, Heavy Horde, Supply Drop, Spawn Menu). Works with other authors' BepInEx mods too.

Needs the ZED ZONE Code Mod Loader (BepInEx), same as our other mods.
